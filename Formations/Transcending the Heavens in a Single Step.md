Allows intergalactic travel between suns by channelling the power of a sun to sustain his transformation and its speed. The formation also directly connects between stars. So travel outside of preset nodes is impossible. 

Originally, the secret function of the formation was also to create a location at the centre of the Solar Array where [[Main Character|Light of Heaven]] could channel a small fraction of all the solar nodes to use their principles to complete his soul flows.

Later in Volume 3, he redesigns the formation to rebuild his body, jump starting his transcension.
