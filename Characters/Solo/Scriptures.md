Old Asian man, long white beard, wears full white outfit, holds a brush for writing his Scriptures.

Uses Ink to create Scripture [[Engraving|Engravings]]. Creating his own principles based on Scripture.
Uses Paper for defences and melee attacks, and ink for offence and long range attacks


# Aspectual Formation 

### Defence
Summons massive papers that absorb projectiles and convert them into writings that describe their flows, allowing him to learn about the attack.
Each paper he summons can only hold one kind of projectile at a time.
The more of the same projectile it absorbs, the faster the deduction occurs. 

### Offence
Launches ink projectiles that engrave weakness/ other effects onto the target
