Guy cultivates the path of annihilation, annihilation is an incomplete thing since to annihilate something is to remove it but therefore it will always leave a gap behind like an outline of what once was. Therefore, in order to counter that one could fill it in with the surrounding material but that would no longer be annihilation but replacement. So true Annihilation instead solves the problem with more annihilation. It destroys anything that can acknowledge the thing that was destroyed and therefore it spreads everything that acknowledges those things that are destroyed, get annihilated themselves and as such, eventually the entire universe ends. This is the path of annihilation.

He was a normal guy, but around a year before the fall of the [[Factions/Organisations/Stability Society]], he grew discontent with his life and sought greater excitement. He learnt about many different paths but none granted him what he desired, until the [[Factions/Organisations/Stability Society]] fell. Witnessing the absolute Annihilation of thm in their entirety gave him the inspiration to create a new path of pure destruction.
