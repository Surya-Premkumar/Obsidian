#Character #Horizon_Walker
Specialises in Strength, sub principle is Space.

Faction Head of the Combat Initiative of the [[Horizon Walkers]]

# Early Life
One of the 6 alongside [[Psychologist]], [[Scientist]] and [[Management]]

# Time in the [[Realm of Horizon|Distant Horizon]]

Alongside the remaining 5, completed the mission in the allocated time of 6 months, but held out longer than most for a year. 

Eventually got married and had a daughter, Hope. 



# Combat Initiative
Leads the Combat Initiative Program of the Horizon Walkers

# Principles

## [[Strength]]
#### Muscle Quality Enhancement
Massively increases the quality of his own muscles beyond human standards

## [[Space]]
#### Muscle Quantity Enhancement
Increases the amount of muscle he can actually have on his body to fit more muscle on him

#### Spatial Rend
Breaks through the space between himself and his opponent to counter Strength's issue of Range.
