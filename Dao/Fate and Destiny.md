Those who can witness fate are few are far between for it is both the now and forever.

Fate is that which has yet to occur in life.

All living beings have a fate and therefore the only way to counter their fate is to die. Ones' fate is as strong as they are, as such, one cannot break their own fate until transcendence. However, can can enforce death upon those who's fates are weaker than their own power
