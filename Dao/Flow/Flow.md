A plan of [[Principle|Principles]] that represent an event/ effect 

[[Person|People]] engrave them onto their souls 


When used by people, Flows are the combination of incomplete flows and [[Principle|Principles]]. A [[Person]] uses the [[Principle|Principles]] inside [[Resources]] and applies them to the 'blueprint' in their soul to create a complete flow/ instance.

In the real world, only complete flows exist and they are perfect from the moment they are instantiated unlike in the soul where a blueprint needs to be tested, and then practiced.
