# Self

A person's self is split into 4 parts
 - Talent for Understanding : The time it takes to comprehend existing flows

 - Ingenuity: The time it takes to create new flows

 - Knowledge: Knowledge they've learnt which allows them to skip the beginning period of time for both understanding & creating flows 

 - Insight: Success rate of the steps of creating or understanding a flow

# Soul

The soul is made of countless uncontrolled flows (like a river). When [[Stage 0|People]] create flows in the soul, it just adds more chaos to the soul usually. Some flows can revolve around themselves and create islands that spiral in on themselves (like whirlpools in the river). These Points are [[Aspects]]. Once an Aspectual Formation is created, itself becomes semi permanent and starts generating principles built directly for the Formation of the soul. This is [[Stage 1]].

### Aspects
If multiple complete flows revolve around a point, that point becomes semi permanent in the non permanent, uncontrollable soul.
Aspects can also be connected to one another since they are still flows. Multiple aspects that all flow into eachother are an Aspectual Formation. The aspects all have to be of the same principle 


### Aspectual Formation 

All are covered by the same principle.

- Offence (Melee, Short, Mid, Long)
- Defence (Passive, Instance, Prolonged Resistance)
- Movement (Passive, Short-Term, Long-Term, Burst/Dodge)
- Reconnaissance (Close Range, Long Range, Remote)
- Healing (Passive, Burst, Short Term)
- Any Utility Aspect (Storage, Communication, idk, other stuff).

[[Painter]] will create his own Aspectual Formation.
